# UX Screen Inventory and Prototype Brief

## P0 prototype screens

1. Sign in and MFA; role-based dashboard.
2. Lead list/detail/follow-up/conversion and customer timeline.
3. Package, itinerary, availability, and pricing.
4. Quotation create/review/send/accept/version history.
5. Booking, cancellation/reschedule, invoice, payment proof/verification/refund request.
6. Vendor, schedule, assignment, and trip board.
7. Customer quotation, payment, and document portal.

Every screen needs desktop/mobile design; loading, empty, error, and access-denied states; validations; destructive-action confirmation; accessibility labels; keyboard journey; analytics event; and link to affected API/resource. Product owner approves journey, exception state, permissions, copy, and acceptance criteria before development.
