# Privacy, Compliance and Data Retention Register

**This is an implementation checklist, not legal advice.**

Before production, obtain qualified review of the legal entity, privacy notice, terms, cancellation/refund terms, consent language, applicable Indonesian tax/accounting/tourism/consumer/data-protection obligations, and data-controller/processor responsibilities for providers.

Maintain a register with data category, purpose, consent/lawful basis, owner, storage, recipient/provider, retention, deletion method, access roles, security controls, and review date. Implement data classification, least privilege, consent withdrawal, data-request workflow, export/deletion workflow, breach escalation, backup retention, vendor review, and quarterly access review.
